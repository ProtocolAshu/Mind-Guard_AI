# Always in control
Allow Once releases the current restriction for one app. Pause Guardian stops all interventions until you resume. Disable for 30 minutes and Disable until tomorrow (06:00 local time) pause protection temporarily. Emergency Override releases every restriction immediately for 24 hours and cannot be blocked by any rule or model.

# Changing your policy
Your Personal AI Constitution is written in plain language and compiled into rules that you review before saving. Every change creates a new policy version, so you can compare and roll back. Rules can never restrict essential apps such as the phone dialer, messages, maps, payments, alarms or emergency services.

# Privacy controls
You can export all of your data, delete all stored history, disable AI analysis, disable content analysis, and change retention. Consent is per scope: usage monitoring, content text analysis, cloud AI reasoning, screenshot analysis, transcript analysis, memory personalization and anonymized research.
